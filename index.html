What I Do

Creative Disciplines

Every project is a chance to explore a different dimension of visual storytelling. Here's where my passion and skills intersect.

01

Film Direction

Crafting narratives that challenge conventional perspectives. From concept to final cut, I direct short films and experimental pieces that find the extraordinary in the mundane.

02

Cinematography

Telling stories through the lens. I obsess over framing, movement, and light to create images that speak before a word is said — turning every frame into meaning.

03

Documentary

Capturing real life with intention. I gravitate toward intimate, observational storytelling — finding the human stories that most people walk past every day.

04

Visual Experimentation

Breaking the rules on purpose. From unconventional editing to mixed-media techniques, my experimental work pushes boundaries and explores what film can become.

About

The Mind Behind
Strange Business

I'm Joseph Leung, a media student at Singapore Polytechnic and an aspiring film director with a deep passion for unconventional storytelling and visual experimentation.

STRANGE BUSINESS isn't just a portfolio name — it's a creative philosophy. I believe the most powerful stories aren't found in the dramatic or the spectacular. They're woven into the fabric of everyday life, hiding in plain sight, waiting for someone to look a little closer.

My work sits at the intersection of filmmaking, documentary, and raw human observation. Every project is an attempt to shift perspective — to make audiences see what was always there, just differently. From intimate character studies to abstract visual experiments, I chase the feeling of discovering something unexpected in the ordinary.

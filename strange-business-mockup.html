About

The Mind Behind
Strange Business

I'm Joseph Leung, a media student at Singapore Polytechnic and an aspiring film director with a passion for unconventional storytelling.

STRANGE BUSINESS isn't just a name — it's a creative philosophy. I believe the most compelling stories aren't found in the dramatic or extraordinary. They're hiding in plain sight, in the ordinary moments most people walk past.

My work sits at the intersection of filmmaking, visual experimentation, and raw human observation. Every project is an attempt to shift perspective — to make people see what was always there, just differently.

"Finding extraordinary stories in ordinary situations — and exploring different perspectives through film."
